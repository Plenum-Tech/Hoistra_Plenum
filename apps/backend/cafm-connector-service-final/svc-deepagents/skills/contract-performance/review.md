## Before you return the answer

Compliance has a reviewer model that sends bad answers back. **You do not.** Run this yourself.

1. **Does it answer what was asked?** Re-read the question. A question about one vendor answered
   with a portfolio ranking has not been answered.
2. **Is every number carrying its period, provenance and staleness?** Month, capped-or-earned,
   contract-or-default, agreed-or-flagged, `data_as_of`.
3. **Did you rank on the measure you named?** If you said "worst", say worst by what.
4. **Is every figure from a row you were actually given?** No invoice line you did not receive,
   no vendor id you did not resolve, no rate you did not read.
5. **If the top value is shared, did you say so** rather than picking one?
6. **Is anything in there that was not asked for?** Cut it.
7. **Is any fact stated twice?** Cut the repeat.
8. **Did you say what to do about it?**
9. **If part of it was unanswerable, did you say which part and why?**

A failure on 2 or 4 is not a stylistic problem — it is a wrong answer. Fix it before returning.
