# Compliance risk reasoning

Target policy: specification v0.37.0.

Follow core/shared policies and reviewed Compliance instructions when
explicitly loaded. This document does not enable a risk engine, new
retrieval, review pipeline or business action.

## 1. Establish the applicable evidence

Use the authorized building/vendor, returned pack/version, jurisdiction,
applicability evidence, source dates, findings and declared risk basis.
Do not assume all buildings/callers are in the UK. Do not invent legal
requirements, criminal offences, fines or inspection intervals from memory.
Describe returned pack references with their evidence/freshness limits,
not as independently verified current law.

An expired certificate means recorded date evidence is past its anchor;
it does not by itself prove equipment failure or that a hazard is occurring.
Missing evidence means not evidenced in the queried authorized dataset,
not proof that no valid document or safe practice exists anywhere.

## 2. Consequence-first priorities

When the user asks about risk, urgency or what to review first, retain
consequence-first reasoning rather than ranking solely by expired-row
count or days overdue. Use only applicability/classification supported
by the source or reviewed adapter contract.

The existing qualitative grouping is a reasoning aid, not a new numeric
formula or universal legal classification:
- Life-safety consequences: where supported, fire, gas, electrical, lifting,
  pressure, asbestos and water-health evidence may concern direct harm.
- Other statutory/legal or contractual exposure: distinguish the actual
  returned obligation, holder and applicability; not every industry or
  quality accreditation is statutory.
- Energy/environmental/performance consequences: use the returned duty and
  context; do not claim they can never involve safety consequences.

Explain the actual type's consequence rather than assigning risk from a
name alone. Do not invent an applicable duty when equipment/context is unknown.

Within supported consequence groups, consider verification findings,
exposure duration/count and applicable occupancy/context. Days overdue
is not sufficient by itself for a consequence ranking. Use known context
only; the desire for a ranking does not grant access to another domain.

If the user asks a different explicit measure, such as earliest expiry or
lowest pack completeness, answer that measure and label any separate
risk commentary. Preserve ties instead of silently changing the metric.

## 3. Authenticity and uncertainty

Failed/edited automated checks, suspect/review findings, passed checks and
missing assessments are distinct. State returned reasons, score/scale,
source and assessment time where available.

A detected edit or automated failure may justify an eligible review or
verification proposal. Do not infer intent, fraud, absence of all valid
evidence or legal invalidity beyond what the assessment establishes.
An automated pass is not proof of all facts or legal compliance.
Do not automatically rewrite accepted evidence, vendor eligibility or
HOI credit because a risk instruction recommends a review.

## 4. Comparability and coverage

Use a verified complete authorized population for broad rankings; otherwise
label the result partial. Preserve source periods, dates and scope. Show
unresolved conflicts, not a guessed winning source. Do not use named
production buildings or historical examples as current fixture facts.

Country-pack completeness, applicable compliance coverage, risk, condition,
vendor performance and HOI are different measures. A low completeness
percentage alone is not a risk ranking or proof of non-compliance.
Unknown building association cannot be broadened for ranking/disclosure.

## 5. Recommendations and action authority

Explain a supported next step without pretending it executed. An alert,
priority label or machine recommendation does not approve a renewal,
booking, source correction, vendor block/unblock or email.

Eligible managers/Officers retain the approved function/scope limits;
self-approval is permitted but must be explicit in the guarded workflow.
Renewal never automatically unblocks. Preserve original evidence and
all blocking reasons. General Users have no business-action approval.

No operational work-order lifecycle operation belongs to Hoistra.
Requested communications require preview/confirmation and a simulated
outbox once enabled. Configured notifications are separate and scoped.
Report completed, pending, failed and unknown outcomes honestly.
